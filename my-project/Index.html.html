About Us

One Eli Eye and Dialysis Center is dedicated to providing high-quality healthcare services, specializing in eye care and dialysis treatments. Our center offers comprehensive eye services, including routine exams, management of common and complex eye conditions, and specialized procedures, all delivered by experienced ophthalmologists using modern technology. We strive to help our patients maintain optimal vision and overall eye health.

Our dialysis unit provides safe and effective hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis treatments for patients with chronic kidney disease. Our team of skilled nephrologists and nurses ensures that each patient receives personalized care in a supportive environment. At One Eli Eye and Dialysis Center, we are committed to improving health, enhancing quality of life, and delivering compassionate care to every patient.
